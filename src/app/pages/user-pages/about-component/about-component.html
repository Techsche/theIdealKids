Ideal Kids

The Ideal Child

The ideal child likes to study when in school, likes to play in the playground, likes to eat at meal-time, likes to sleep at bed-time, always full of love for all those around them, and full of confidence in the divine Grace.

Good-tempered

Does not become angry when things seem to go against them or decisions are not in their favor.

Game

Whatever they do, to the best of their capacity and keeps on doing in the face of almost certain failure. Always thinks straight and acts straight.

Truthful

Never fears to say the truth whatever may be the consequences.

Patient

Does not get disheartened if they have to wait a long time to see the results of their efforts.

Enduring

Faces the inevitable difficulties and suffering without grumbling.

Persevering

Never slackens their effort however long it has to last.

Poised

Keeps equanimity in success as well as in failure.

Courageous

Always goes on fighting for the final victory though they may meet with many defeats.

Cheerful

Knows how to smile and keep a happy heart in all circumstance.

Modest

Does not become conceited over their success, neither does feel themselves superior to their comrades.

Generous

Appreciates the merits of others and is always ready to help another to succeed.

Fair and Obedient

Observes the discipline and is always honest.

What is our objective?

All kids are very unique and talented on many aspects. Ideal Kids organization encourages the kids and creates an opportunity to show their talents and help them to develop their skills.

How we can achieve it?

We organize many events on several occasions throughout the year to help and build the skills from among our kids. Please review the events section to see more details.
