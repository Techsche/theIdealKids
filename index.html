Welcome to The Ideal Kids

The Ideal Kids is a non-profit organization under 501 (c) (3) of the Internal Revenue Code. Since 2010, we have been organizing events that nurture children to become well-balanced individuals — loving learning, enjoying play, following healthy routines, and growing with confidence and values.
